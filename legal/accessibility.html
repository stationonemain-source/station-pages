Station is committed to making station.solutions usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We treat accessibility as an ongoing part of how we build and maintain this website, not a one-time checkbox.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and by accessibility law in most jurisdictions. We test against these guidelines as we build and update the site.

What we've done

  • Keyboard navigation — every interactive control (menus, buttons, forms, the cart, the chat) is reachable and operable with a keyboard, and shows a visible focus outline.
  • Screen readers — semantic HTML, descriptive labels on buttons and form fields, and ARIA attributes where native semantics fall short.
  • Contrast — text and essential controls are chosen to meet AA contrast ratios against their backgrounds.
  • Reduced motion — animations respect your operating system's "reduce motion" setting and turn off when you ask them to.
  • Responsive & zoom — the site reflows cleanly on small screens and remains usable when text is enlarged.
  • Alternatives — anything you can do on the site you can also do by phone or email with a human.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is never "finished." Some third-party components (such as our booking widget and payment pages) are provided by vendors and may not fully match our standard; we choose vendors with accessibility in mind and will help you complete anything you can't do on your own. If you find a barrier anywhere on this site, we want to fix it.
